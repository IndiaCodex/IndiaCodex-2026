# Slide 9 — Commercial Potential

## Title

The layer that turns "can agents touch money" into "yes"

## Content

- **Buyer, in two stages:** agent developers adopt it first (free debugging
  superpower — deterministic replay of a hung or failed run); compliance
  and risk teams become the budget holder once agents move real money
- **What's monetizable:** hosted storage at scale, artifact signing and
  compliance tooling, enterprise auth/multi-tenancy — the exact items
  already named as "not built yet" on the roadmap
- **What stays open:** the capture → replay → verify → explain pipeline
  itself, MIT licensed, to maximize the number of executions captured in
  this format across the Masumi ecosystem

## Speaker Notes

Frame it as risk reduction, not a feature list: "A provable, tamper-evident
record of autonomous financial actions is the specific thing that turns
'can we let agents touch money' from a no into a yes for a CTO. That's a
deployment blocker removed, which is worth paying for the same way
companies already pay for Datadog or a SOC2 audit tool — not because a hash
chain is exciting, but because the alternative is a board-level risk." Keep
this slide short; commercial framing should feel like one clear idea, not a
pitch deck within a pitch deck.

## Visual Suggestions

A simple two-tier diagram: "Open core" (capture/journal/replay/verify/
explain/export, MIT) at the base, "Commercial surface" (hosted scale,
signing, compliance, multi-tenant auth) as a thinner layer on top —
communicates the strategy in one glance without needing bullet points.
