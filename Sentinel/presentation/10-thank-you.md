## Title

The assurance layer Masumi doesn't have yet

## Content

## Speaker Notes

Close on the install command, not a sales line — it's a more credible ending
than "thank you" alone because it's an invitation to verify everything just
said, immediately, with nothing hidden. "We're not going to tell you this is
finished. What's here today is a real, tested, working assurance pipeline
with a live Masumi integration, not a mockup of one. Try it yourselves —
pnpm install, pnpm demo, two minutes. Thank you, and we'd genuinely like
your questions." Stop talking after that sentence; let the silence invite
the first question rather than filling it.

## Visual Suggestions

Large terminal-styled block with the two-line install command as the visual
centerpiece — nothing else competing for attention. License badge and a
one-line repo pointer beneath it, small.
