## Speaker Notes

Ten seconds, no more. Introduce yourself and the one-line premise, then move —
this slide's job is orientation, not persuasion: "We're Team [name], and this
is Sentinel — the engineering assurance layer for autonomous agents built on
Masumi." Don't explain what "assurance layer" means yet; the next slide earns
that.

concept deck, before you've said a word.
