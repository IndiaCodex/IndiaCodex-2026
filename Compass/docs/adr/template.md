## Context

What problem or set of forces makes this decision necessary. State the situation neutrally — describe the tension or tradeoff, not the conclusion. A reader should be able to reach their own view from this section before reading the decision.

## Decision

What was decided, stated plainly and specifically enough that someone could verify whether it's been followed.

## Alternatives Considered

What else was on the table, and why each was rejected. This is often the most valuable part of the record — it's what stops the same rejected alternative from being re-proposed later without the context for why it didn't work.

## Consequences

What this decision makes easier, what it makes harder, and what it forecloses. Be honest about the costs, not just the benefits — a decision with no stated downside wasn't examined closely enough.
